## Goal

Refine the milestone deliverable editor after hands-on use so it feels fully native to the task workspace rather than merely sharing its shell.

## Scope

- Review information density, spacing, typography, and responsive behavior in light and dark themes.
- Refine the relationship between deliverable content, milestone properties, and activation-gate controls.
- Improve contextual editing affordances, empty states, loading and error presentation, and keyboard flow.
- Preserve the established frosted task-dialog surface and content-first interaction grammar.

## Acceptance criteria

- The editor has been reviewed with realistic descriptions and activation-gate sets at desktop and mobile widths.
- Primary deliverable content remains visually dominant while gate controls stay clear and usable.
- Editing, confirmation, empty, loading, error, disabled, and read-only states are coherent and accessible.
- Visual changes are covered by focused component or Storybook interaction tests and browser validation.
