Follow the Milestone Deliverables and Activation Triggers architecture wiki entry.

Add application-service and CLI workflows for listing, creating, renaming, and removing activation trigger definitions and for attaching or detaching triggers from milestones.

Acceptance criteria:
- Definition mutations validate references and activation graph impact before writing.
- Removing a consumed trigger or referenced source is rejected.
- Requirement edits are allowed through ordinary update only while the trigger is inactive.
- Attaching an active trigger immediately satisfies that gate for its new consumer.
- Impact summaries identify affected milestones.
- Commands use established AppResult and Spectre.Console patterns with focused tests.

Active-trigger redefinition is handled separately.
