## Goal

Test whether the proposed vocabulary tells a useful story for materially different kinds of PM projects.

## Validate

- Compose fixtures for a software product, library, infrastructure repository, and small personal project.
- Exercise minimal implicit content and richer explicit configurations.
- Review realistic desktop and mobile density, light and dark themes, keyboard flow, semantic structure, contrast, empty states, and long content.
- Confirm that configuration changes content and ordering without creating a page builder.
- Record rejected ideas, unresolved problems, and any vocabulary changes required by the review.

## Deliverable

An approved prototype composition and concise review record identifying the final section vocabulary and defaults.
