Follow the Milestone Deliverables and Activation Triggers architecture wiki entry.

Extend local read models with resolved trigger, milestone lifecycle, and task activation eligibility, then apply eligibility before dependency readiness and ranking.

Acceptance criteria:
- Board, navigation, task detail, and next-task reads expose trustworthy activation summaries.
- Inactive and delivered milestone tasks remain visible in ordinary reads.
- Next-task excludes inactive and delivered milestone tasks before ready-only filtering and ranking.
- Include-blocked never bypasses activation eligibility.
- Scoped no-result reasons identify unmet activation gates.
- Board and resource revisions change when activation or delivery state affects visible results.
- Focused deterministic ranking tests preserve the existing ordering after eligibility filtering.
