Follow the Milestone Deliverables and Activation Triggers architecture wiki entry.

Integrate each owning project's local activation model into linked-project and family task recommendation flows.

Acceptance criteria:
- Candidates are filtered by their owning project's milestone lifecycle before dependency enrichment and federated ranking.
- Linked projects do not inherit activation triggers, milestones, delivery state, or authority.
- Local-only activation sources remain enforced.
- Unavailable linked projects produce existing structured warnings without corrupting local evaluation.
- Selected task reasons include activation context where useful.
- Integration tests cover current, selected linked, and family scopes.
