Follow the Milestone Deliverables and Activation Triggers architecture wiki entry.

Extend project validation and mutation-level validation for structured milestones, trigger definitions, activation records, and delivery records.

Acceptance criteria:
- Reject duplicate requirements and duplicate trigger references.
- Reject missing sources and unknown local task, milestone, or trigger references.
- Reject invalid activation mode and field combinations.
- Require override reasons and exact waived-requirement snapshots.
- Reject invalid ordinary or exceptional delivery records.
- Report unused triggers and empty milestones as non-blocking findings where specified.
- Use stable structured issue codes with focused tests.

Activation cycle detection is handled separately.
