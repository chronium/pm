## Goal

Turn the approved discovery work into a decision-complete production boundary without implementing it in this milestone.

## Consolidate

- Record the final configuration schema, validation rules, route behavior, selection algorithms, read model, and presentation states.
- Capture the accepted Storybook compositions and accessibility/responsive expectations.
- Identify required .NET, API, snapshot, Angular, publishing, documentation, migration, and test changes.
- Remove superseded discovery alternatives from the proposed production path.
- After owner approval, create a separate implementation milestone and appropriately sized tasks.

## Deliverable

An implementation-ready design and a separately approved production milestone whose tasks do not need to reopen foundational product decisions.
