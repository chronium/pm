## Goal

Bring the Angular wiki create, body-edit, and metadata workflows up to the quality and restraint of the completed wiki reader experience.

## Scope

- Refine the full-page create, body-edit, and metadata layouts for clearer hierarchy, denser use of space, and consistent alignment with the reader.
- Improve EasyMDE integration, toolbar presentation, editor sizing, focus behavior, keyboard flow, and responsive/mobile behavior.
- Make save, cancel, dirty-navigation, loading, validation, duplicate-path, stale-conflict, reload-latest, rename, and confirmed-delete states visually clear without adding decorative chrome.
- Keep body editing Markdown-only and metadata editing limited to path/title; do not expose frontmatter or local-file details.
- Reuse the established design tokens, buttons, form fields, confirmation dialog, Markdown components, and full-page wiki patterns.
- Preserve drafts on failures and 412 conflicts, and keep destructive actions contextual and confirmable.
- Add focused component and Storybook coverage for default, dirty, pending, error, conflict, disabled, long-content, and mobile states.
- Use Playwright for final keyboard, responsive, and critical mutation smoke validation.

## Acceptance

- Create, body edit, metadata rename, and deletion feel cohesive with the wiki reader and remain efficient at realistic content lengths.
- The editor is accessible by keyboard, has visible focus, and does not lose local drafts during validation or conflict handling.
- Mobile layouts preserve primary actions and avoid accidental horizontal scrolling.
- Relevant Angular unit tests, Storybook tests, strict build, production build, and browser smoke checks pass.

## Out of scope

- Wiki search, MCP outline/patch operations, backend API changes, and changes to the reader presentation.
