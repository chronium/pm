Follow the Milestone Deliverables and Activation Triggers architecture wiki entry.

Create the Angular activation switchboard for inspecting requirements, provenance, consumers, and contextual control-plane actions.

Acceptance criteria:
- Show current satisfied counts separately from activation mode and historical waived requirements.
- Expand task and milestone requirements with navigable source links.
- Offer only contextually valid Activate, Override, Reset, Redefine, and Reconcile actions.
- Require reasons and impact confirmation where defined.
- Do not expose controls in static or read-only contexts.
- Support keyboard operation, accessible names, localized pending/error feedback, and realistic data density.
- Add component, store, dialog, and Storybook tests.
