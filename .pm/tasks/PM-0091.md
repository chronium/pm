Follow the Milestone Deliverables and Activation Triggers architecture wiki entry.

Extend the Angular settings experience so milestones are edited as deliverables rather than title-only buckets.

Acceptance criteria:
- Edit title, Markdown description, inherited priority, and required activation triggers.
- Present description guidance around outcome, scope, exclusions, and evidence.
- Preserve dense layout, keyboard navigation, visible focus, and revision conflict handling.
- Preview eligibility impact before changing required trigger consumption.
- Support loading, validation, read-only, and error states.
- Add focused component, store, and Storybook coverage.
